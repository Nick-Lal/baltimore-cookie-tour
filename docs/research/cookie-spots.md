# Baltimore chocolate chip cookie research

Compiled 16 August 2026. Fourteen stops, all inside city limits, all geocoded against
OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service. Addresses and hours come from the businesses' own sites
where they publish them, and from Yelp or Toast ordering pages where they don't.

## How to read the confidence column

Plenty of Baltimore bakeries are excellent without having a chocolate chip cookie on the
counter every day. Because that distinction matters for a cookie tour, every stop below
carries a confidence rating about the chocolate chip cookie specifically, not about the
bakery in general.

- **Confirmed** means a named chocolate chip cookie appears on the shop's own menu, or a
  published review describes eating one there.
- **Likely** means the shop sells cookies and multiple reviews mention chocolate chip, but
  the shop's own menu rotates and doesn't guarantee it.
- **Rotating** means the bakery bakes a changing daily list and chocolate chip shows up some
  days and not others. Call ahead.

Nothing in this file replaces going and looking. That is the point of the tour.

## The stops

### North cluster: Hampden and Remington

**Ovenbird Bakery, The Rotunda** — 711 W 40th St, 21211 — 39.3367, -76.6299 — *Confirmed*

Ovenbird is a sourdough bakery first, run by Keiller Kyle, and the cookie is the thing people
come back for anyway. It's called the Chocolate ChiRp: Belgian milk chocolate shaved into the
dough rather than dropped in as chips, finished with Maldon salt. The shaving matters. You get
chocolate in thin sheets through the crumb instead of discrete molten pockets, which is a real
technique difference worth scoring against the others. This is the newest of Ovenbird's three
locations and the one that put them within reach of Hampden, Roland Park, and Homewood.

**Harmony Bakery** — 3446 Chestnut Ave, 21211 — 39.3298, -76.6295 — *Confirmed*

Entirely vegan, gluten free, and soy free, with flours milled and blended in house. Whether
that helps or hurts a chocolate chip cookie is exactly the kind of question a scoring rubric
should be able to answer, so Harmony earns its place as the control case for anyone who
assumes the answer already. They also do pizza and spinach pies, so it works as a real stop
rather than a two minute counter visit.

**Common Ground** — 3543 Chestnut Ave, 21211 — 39.3309, -76.6290 — *Confirmed*

A worker cooperative café 225 metres up Chestnut Avenue from Harmony, baking its own cookies, scones and
muffins from scratch every morning. Less precious than the pastry shops on this list and
cheaper than all of them, which makes it a useful test of whether technique or plain freshness
is doing the work. Open 7am to 3pm, closed Wednesdays, which catches people out.

This entry replaced Maillard Patisserie, which used to sit two doors from Harmony and closed in
May 2026. See the closures section below.

### Remington and Charles Village

**La Maison by Café Dear Leon** — 2600 N Howard St, 21218 — 39.3194, -76.6203 — *Likely*

The Remington sibling of the Canton café, and by far the biggest room on this list: 5,000
square feet, 155 seats inside and another 68 on the patio. A Korean American take on a French
boulangerie. Open 7:30am to 3pm daily. Better known for its s'mores cookie than its plain
chocolate chip, so treat the chocolate chip as a good bet rather than a guarantee.

### Central cluster: Mount Vernon and downtown

**Aunt Kelly's Cookies** — 857 N Howard St, 21201 — 39.2999, -76.6198 — *Confirmed*

A cookie shop, not a bakery that also sells cookies, which makes it the closest thing this
list has to a benchmark. Black owned, sitting on Mount Vernon's Antique Row, baking daily.
Butter crunch is the famous one and chocolate chip is the one people argue about. Open Tuesday
through Saturday, 11am to 5pm, closed Sunday and Monday. Those hours will shape your route
more than anything else on this list.

**Patisserie Poupon** — 820 E Baltimore St, 21202 — 39.2903, -76.6048 — *Rotating*

A genuinely French pâtisserie that has been downtown for decades. Cookies are on the menu but
the house is built on canelés, croissants, and tiramisu. Include it if you want a stop that
tests whether classical French technique produces a better American cookie. Sometimes the
answer is no, and that is a finding.

### Southeast cluster: Little Italy and Fells Point

**Ovenbird Bakery, Little Italy** — 300 S Exeter St, 21202 — 39.2866, -76.6009 — *Confirmed*

The original. Same Chocolate ChiRp as the Rotunda location, which sets up the most useful
control in the whole tour: same recipe, different oven, different day, different baker on
shift. If your scores diverge between the two Ovenbirds, that tells you something about the
precision of your own palate before it tells you anything about the cookie. Open 8am to 4pm
Monday through Saturday, 8am to 3pm Sunday.

**Kneads Bakeshop** — 506 S Central Ave, 21202 — 39.2851, -76.5991 — *Confirmed*

An artisan bakeshop and café doing handcrafted breads and a proper lunch menu, open seven days
a week from 7am to 5pm. That makes it the most reliable stop on this list: no rotating daily
list, no early close, chocolate chip and chocolate chip chunk on the counter every day. Three
hundred metres from Ovenbird's Little Italy shop, which makes the two an easy back to back
comparison of an American bakeshop against a sourdough house.

**Pitango Bakery + Café** — 903 S Ann St, 21231 — 39.2815, -76.5910 — *Rotating*

On a quiet waterfront side street in Fells Point, which makes it the best sitting-down stop on
the list. Italian leaning: charcuterie, sandwiches, breads baked daily. Chocolate chip cookies
appear on some days and not others. Worth the risk for the setting alone if you need a place
to actually talk instead of eating standing up.

**Sacré Sucré** — 933 Fell St, 21231 — 39.2812, -76.5899 — *Rotating*

Macarons are the specialty and the reason the place outgrew its old Fleet Street shop and
moved into a much larger waterfront space in 2024. It sells cookies, including in gift boxes,
but a chocolate chip cookie is not the headline. Two hundred metres from Pitango, so there is
no real cost to checking both.

### East cluster: Canton and Highlandtown

**Café Dear Leon** — 2929 O'Donnell St, 21224 — 39.2799, -76.5743 — *Likely*

The original location, on O'Donnell Square. Strong pastry program, and the s'mores cookie is
the one that shows up in photos. Good coffee, which matters more than it sounds like it does
when you are five cookies deep and need something to reset your palate.

**Ovenbird Bakery, Highlandtown** — 3925 Gough St #2, 21224 — 39.2885, -76.5639 — *Confirmed*

The third Ovenbird and the one with the longest hours: 8am to 7pm on weekdays. If your date
starts late, this is often the only serious bakery still open. Same Chocolate ChiRp.

### South cluster: Federal Hill

**Zoe's Just Dezzerts** — 828 S Charles St, 21230 — 39.2791, -76.6148 — *Likely*

The only Federal Hill stop still standing after a rough couple of years for bakeries down here.
Cakes, cupcakes, tarts and cookies, with allergy friendly options that go further than most.
Open half twelve to half six, closed Monday and Tuesday, so this is an end of tour stop rather
than a start. It replaced Dulceology, three blocks south, which closed in May 2026.

### Late night

**Insomnia Cookies, Charles Village** — 3120 St Paul St, 21218 — 39.3266, -76.6167 — *Confirmed*

A chain, and included deliberately. It is open until 3am near Hopkins, it sells a warm
chocolate chip cookie for about two dollars, and it is the industry baseline that every
independent bakery on this list is implicitly competing against. Scoring it honestly is more
useful than pretending it doesn't exist. If a nine dollar artisan cookie can't beat it on your
own rubric, that result is worth knowing.

## Two Baltimore classics that are not stops

**Berger Cookies** are a vanilla wafer under a slab of chocolate fudge. Sold in every grocery
store in the region, iconic, and not a chocolate chip cookie by any definition. Out of scope.

**Otterbein's** has been baking in Baltimore since 1881, and chocolate chip is their best
selling flavor. The cookies are wafer thin and sold in bags at supermarkets rather than at a
counter you can visit. Worth buying a bag as a texture reference at the opposite end of the
spectrum from Ovenbird, but there is no storefront to route to.

## Two places that closed while this was being researched

Both of these were on the list, with addresses and hours, before a verification pass caught
them. Neither is a stop. They are recorded here because a cookie tour that sends you to a
locked door is worse than one with fewer stops.

**Maillard Patisserie**, 3528 Chestnut Ave, closed in early May 2026. Both the Hampden shop and
the Hamilton one shut at the same time, with the owner citing the workload of running a small
business. Common Ground, a hundred metres up the same street, took its place here.

**Dulceology Bakery**, 1138 S Charles St, closed its Federal Hill storefront on 17 May 2026
after six years. Zoe's Just Dezzerts, three blocks north, took its place.

The lesson for anyone using this list months from now: bakeries close. Ring ahead, and treat
the confidence ratings as a starting point rather than a promise.

## Geographic notes for routing

The stops fall into seven clusters. Within a cluster everything is a short walk. Between
clusters you need wheels, and that is where the transport picker earns its keep.

Fells Point is the densest pair: Pitango and Sacré Sucré are 200 metres apart on the walking
network. Chestnut Avenue in Hampden is next, with Harmony and Common Ground 225 metres apart,
then Harbor East, where Ovenbird and Kneads are 300 metres apart. The longest gap between any
two clusters is Hampden to Highlandtown, just under 10 km, which is a 40 minute scooter ride and
not a walk anyone should attempt on a date.

Every figure here comes from the committed walking matrix in `data/matrix.json` rather than from
eyeballing a map.

Three hard constraints shape any route. Aunt Kelly's closes at 5 and shuts Sunday and Monday.
Zoe's does not open until half twelve. Common Ground closes on Wednesdays. A tour that includes
all three has to run Thursday to Saturday, start in the morning, and move roughly north to
south.

If you want the least fragile version of this tour, three stops carry no timing risk at all:
Kneads is open 7 to 5 every day, Ovenbird Highlandtown runs to 7 on weekdays, and Pitango goes
until 9 or later. That is a whole evening's worth of cookies with nothing to plan around.
